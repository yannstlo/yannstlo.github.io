Privacy Policy

Photo Calendar Maker

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Photos and projects stay under your control

Photo Calendar Maker for Mac, iPhone, and iPad stores the photos you import and your calendar layouts locally on your device. The app processes photos and generates PDFs on your device. It does not upload your photos or projects to servers operated by us.

Photo access

The app uses the system photo picker to let you choose photos to import. It stores copies of the selected photos in your calendar project. Deleting a project or an imported photo in the app does not delete the original from your Photos library.

Exporting and sharing

You can save or share exported PDFs using your device's file and sharing tools. Files you choose to send to another app, cloud storage provider, printer, or person leave the app at your request. Those services handle the files under their own privacy policies. Device backups may also include app data, depending on your system settings. Version 1.0 does not provide in-app iCloud project sync.

No analytics or tracking

The app does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking tools. We do not collect your photos, calendar projects, or app usage data through the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete imported photos and projects from within the app. Copies you have exported or shared, temporary preview files opened in other apps, and system backups are managed separately through those apps, storage locations, or device settings.

Support and contact

If you contact hello@yann.ca, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private photos or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your support request.
