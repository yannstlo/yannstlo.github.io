Crispy Honey-Soy Chicken Bowls with Sriracha Mayo

Golden, crispy chicken tossed in a glossy honey-garlic soy glaze, served over rice with roasted broccoli and carrots. The finishing touch is the sriracha-lime mayo: creamy, tangy and just spicy enough.

Ingredients

Rice and roasted vegetables

  • 300 g (1½ cups) uncooked white rice
  • 400 g (about 4 cups) broccoli florets
  • 300 g (3 medium) carrots
  • 1 tbsp (15 ml) neutral oil
  • ¼ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Crispy chicken

  • 600 g boneless chicken breasts, cut into 2 cm (¾ in) pieces
  • ¼ cup (32 g) cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp (30 ml) neutral oil, divided
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Honey-garlic soy glaze

  • 3 tbsp (45 ml) reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp (42 g) honey
  • 2 tbsp (30 ml) unseasoned rice vinegar
  • ¼ cup (60 ml) water
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1 tsp (3 g) cornstarch

Sriracha-lime mayo

  • 2 tbsp (30 g) mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp (5 ml) sriracha
  • 1 lime use 1 tsp juice for the mayo; cut the rest into wedges

Directions

  1. Start the oven and rice. Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan / 425°F. Cook 300 g (1½ cups) white rice with the water amount and cooking time on its packet. Rest covered.
  2. Roast the vegetables. Slice 300 g carrots into 5 mm (¼ in) diagonals. Toss with 400 g broccoli, 1 tbsp (15 ml) neutral oil, ¼ tsp salt and ¼ tsp black pepper. Spread out on a large tray; use two if crowded. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and browned.
  3. Make the sriracha-lime mayo. Mix 2 tbsp (30 g) mayonnaise with 1 tsp (5 ml) sriracha and 1 tsp lime juice. Cut the rest of the lime into wedges and set aside for serving.
  4. Mix the honey-garlic soy glaze. Whisk together 3 tbsp (45 ml) reduced-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp (42 g) honey, 2 tbsp (30 ml) unseasoned rice vinegar, ¼ cup (60 ml) water, 4 finely minced garlic cloves and 1 tsp (3 g) cornstarch. Set aside.
  5. Coat the chicken. Pat 600 g chicken breasts very dry and cut into 2 cm (¾ in) pieces. Just before frying, toss with ¼ cup (32 g) cornstarch and ¼ tsp black pepper for a thin coating.
  6. Pan-fry in two batches. Heat 1 tbsp (15 ml) neutral oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add 300 g of the coated chicken in one layer. Cook for 6–8 minutes, turning, until golden and the thickest pieces reach 74°C / 165°F on a food thermometer. Set on a rack, uncovered. Repeat with the remaining 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil and 300 g coated chicken.
  7. Glaze just before serving. Lower the heat to medium and remove any burnt crumbs. Re-whisk and add all the prepared glaze: 3 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, ¼ cup water, 4 garlic cloves and 1 tsp cornstarch. Simmer for 1–2 minutes until glossy. Turn off the heat and toss in both batches of cooked chicken (from 600 g raw chicken) for about 20 seconds.
  8. Build four bowls and drizzle. Divide the cooked rice (from 300 g / 1½ cups dry), roasted 400 g broccoli, roasted 300 g carrots and all the glazed chicken among 4 bowls. Drizzle with the sriracha mayo made from 2 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tsp sriracha and 1 tsp lime juice. Serve with the remaining lime wedges and eat right away.

Keep the crunch

  • Pat the chicken very dry, coat it just before frying and cook it in two batches so it browns instead of steaming.
  • Keep the cooked chicken uncovered on a rack. Add the glaze off the heat for just 20 seconds, then serve immediately.
  • Don't skip the sriracha-lime mayo: 2 tbsp mayonnaise + 1 tsp sriracha + 1 tsp lime juice is the finishing drizzle for all four bowls.
  • The 35–40 minute timing assumes white rice, a large skillet and cooking the rice and vegetables while you pan-fry.
  • Check the thickest chicken pieces with a food thermometer: 74°C / 165°F.
