Slow-Cooker Beef Bourguignon

Tender beef in a rich red-wine sauce with carrots, bacon, mushrooms and pearl onions. Do the real work the night before, let it cook while you're hiking, then stir in the browned mushrooms and pearl onions when you get home.

Ingredients

  • 3 lb / 1.4 kg beef chuck, cut into 1½-inch cubes
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3–4 carrots, cut into thick pieces
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups dry red wine
  • 1½ cups beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, or several fresh sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 lb / 450 g small mushrooms, halved or quartered
  • 1½ cups pearl onions, peeled (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Chopped parsley, for serving
  • Optional: 1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp cold water

Directions

  1. Brown the bacon. Cook the chopped bacon in a large pan until it starts to crisp. Transfer it to the slow cooker, leaving the fat in the pan.
  2. Brown the beef. Pat the beef dry, season with salt and pepper, and brown it in batches in the bacon fat. Transfer to the slow cooker.
  3. Build the sauce. In the same pan, cook the yellow onion and carrots for 4–5 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste and cook 1 minute. Sprinkle over the flour and stir for 1 minute. Pour in the wine, scrape up the browned bits and simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Assemble for tomorrow. Pour the pan mixture into the slow cooker with the beef and bacon. Add broth, Worcestershire, thyme and bay leaves. Cool before refrigerating. If your slow-cooker manual permits refrigerating the removable insert, cover and refrigerate it overnight; otherwise refrigerate the mixture in a covered container and transfer it in the morning.
  5. Prep the finishing vegetables separately. Melt the butter in a clean skillet. Brown the mushrooms, then add the pearl onions for the last few minutes. Cool, cover and refrigerate separately overnight.
  6. Cook while you're out. In the morning, place the cold insert into a cold base if your model permits it, or transfer the chilled mixture to the cooker. Cook on LOW for 7–8 hours; allow roughly 8–9 hours when starting refrigerator-cold.
  7. Add the mushrooms and pearl onions. When you get home, stir the separately sautéed mushrooms and pearl onions into the slow cooker. Let them heat through for 10–15 minutes.
  8. Finish and serve. Remove bay leaves and any thyme stems. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. If the sauce is thinner than you want, stir together 1 tbsp cornstarch and 1 tbsp cold water, add it, switch to HIGH and cook about 10 minutes. Garnish with parsley.

Cook mode puts the quantities exactly where you need them. The first five steps are ideal for the night before; the morning is basically fridge → slow cooker → LOW → go hiking.

Notes

  • The mushrooms and pearl onions stay much nicer when browned separately and added only for the final 10–15 minutes.
  • A drinkable, inexpensive Pinot Noir, Merlot or Cabernet Sauvignon works well. Save the fancy bottle for the table.
  • Serve with mashed potatoes or a crusty baguette and a simple green salad.
  • If your slow-cooker manufacturer says not to refrigerate the removable crock, refrigerate the prepared mixture in a separate container instead.
