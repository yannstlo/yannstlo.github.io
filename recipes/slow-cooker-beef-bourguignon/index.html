Slow-Cooker Beef Bourguignon

Tender slow-cooker beef bourguignon in a rich red-wine sauce with carrots, bacon, mushrooms and pearl onions. Brown the beef, build the sauce, then slow-cook until tender. Add freshly sautéed mushrooms and pearl onions at the end.

Yann's rating: ★★★★★
Prep: 30 min Cook: 7–8 hr LOW Serves: 6–8 Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 lb / 1.4 kg beef chuck, cut into 1½-inch cubes
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 3–4 carrots, cut into thick pieces
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups dry red wine
  • 1½ cups beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, or several fresh sprigs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 lb / 450 g small mushrooms, halved or quartered
  • 1½ cups pearl onions, peeled (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Chopped parsley, for serving
  • Optional: 1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp cold water

Directions

  1. Brown the bacon. Cook 6 slices chopped bacon in a large pan until it starts to crisp. Transfer it to the slow cooker, leaving the fat in the pan.
  2. Brown the beef. Pat 3 lb / 1.4 kg beef chuck dry, season with 1 tsp salt and ½ tsp black pepper, and brown it in batches in the bacon fat. Transfer each batch to the slow cooker.
  3. Build the sauce. In the same pan, cook 1 large diced yellow onion and 3–4 carrots, cut into thick pieces, for 4–5 minutes. Add 4 minced garlic cloves and 2 tbsp tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Stir in 3 tbsp flour for 1 minute. Pour in 2 cups dry red wine, scrape up the browned bits and simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Assemble and start cooking. Pour the pan mixture over the beef and bacon. Add 1½ cups beef broth, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, 1 tsp dried thyme (or several fresh sprigs) and 2 bay leaves. Stir, cover and start cooking immediately on LOW for 7–8 hours, until the beef is fork-tender.
  5. Sauté the finishing vegetables. Near the end of the cooking time, melt 2 tbsp butter in a clean skillet. Brown 1 lb / 450 g mushrooms, halved or quartered, then add 1½ cups peeled pearl onions and cook until tender and lightly browned.
  6. Add the mushrooms and pearl onions. Stir all of the freshly sautéed mushrooms and pearl onions into the slow cooker for the final 10–15 minutes.
  7. Finish and serve. Remove the 2 bay leaves and any thyme stems. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. For a thicker sauce, mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp cold water, stir it in, switch to HIGH and cook for about 10 minutes. Garnish with chopped parsley and serve.

Notes

  • The mushrooms and pearl onions stay much nicer when browned separately and added only for the final 10–15 minutes.
  • A drinkable, inexpensive Pinot Noir, Merlot or Cabernet Sauvignon works well. Save the fancy bottle for the table.
  • Serve with mashed potatoes or a crusty baguette and a simple green salad.
  • Use thawed beef and proceed directly from browning and sauce-making to slow cooking. Follow your slow-cooker manufacturer’s fill-level and operating guidance.
