Lemon-Garlic Dijon Chicken Thighs

Boneless, skin-on chicken thighs baked in a bright Dijon, honey, garlic and lemon sauce. Easy enough for a weeknight, but the crisp skin and buttery sauce make it feel much fancier.

Yann's rating: ★★★★★
Prep: 10 min Cook: 25–30 min Serves: 4–6 Difficulty: Easy
Chicken Dinner Easy Oven

Ingredients

  • 6 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1½ tbsp honey
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp melted butter or olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F / 220°C.
  2. Pat the chicken dry, especially the skin. Season both sides with salt, pepper and paprika.
  3. Mix the garlic, Dijon, honey, lemon juice, melted butter and oregano.
  4. Arrange the thighs skin-side up in a baking dish or on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
  5. Spoon the sauce mostly around and underneath the chicken. Keep the skin mostly uncovered so it can crisp.
  6. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until cooked through.
  7. If the skin needs more colour, broil for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Serve with a squeeze of fresh lemon.

Notes

  • Keeping the sauce mostly under and around the thighs helps the skin crisp instead of steaming.
  • Great with roasted baby potatoes, green beans, broccoli or mixed vegetables.
  • The broiler is useful at the end, but keep an eye on the edges because the honey can caramelize fast.
