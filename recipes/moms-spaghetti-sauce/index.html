A note on the handwritten amounts

The amounts preserve the handwritten recipe. Its soup-can size is absent; 284 mL per can is an assumption. The garlic-powder and bay-leaf lines carry handwritten question marks. Bay leaves are confirmed whole; the written teaspoon amount and leaf count still need confirmation. The mixed-spice blend and clove form are unspecified. Confirm these details before cooking; whole and ground spices are not interchangeable.

Keep whole bay leaves and any whole cloves in a food-safe removable spice bag and take it out before serving. Do not leave whole cloves or bay-leaf pieces in the finished sauce.

5 lb beef + 5 lb pork

Full original recipe

The original quantities, retained as written. Use a very large pot with enough room for the ingredients and stirring.

Ingredients

Meat and vegetables

  • 5 lb lean ground beef
  • 5 lb lean ground pork
  • 2–3 red bell peppers, chopped
  • 2 large onions, chopped
  • Garlic, to taste
  • Other vegetables, to taste
  • Oil, enough to coat the bottom of the pot

Seasonings

  • 4 tbsp salt
  • 1½ tsp cayenne
  • 1 tsp crushed pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1½ tbsp curry powder
  • 4 tbsp garlic powder — ? in the original
  • 2 tsp mixed spices — blend unspecified
  • 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tsp cloves — form unspecified
  • 2 tsp whole bay leaves — ? in the original; written volume needs confirmation
  • 1 tbsp basil; fresh basil as desired

Tomato products and juice

  • 3 × 680 mL cans (2,040 mL) Hunt’s tomato sauce
  • 3 cans Aylmer tomato soup — 284 mL each assumed
  • 3 × 156 mL cans (468 mL) tomato paste
  • 2 × 945 mL containers (1,890 mL) Garden Cocktail vegetable juice

Directions

  1. Prepare everything first. Chop 2–3 red bell peppers, 2 large onions, garlic to taste and any additional vegetables. Open or measure 3 × 680 mL cans tomato sauce, 3 cans tomato soup (284 mL each assumed), 3 × 156 mL cans tomato paste and 2 × 945 mL containers vegetable juice. Have everything ready before starting the meat.
  2. Measure the seasonings. Measure 4 tbsp salt, 1½ tsp cayenne, 1 tsp crushed pepper flakes, 2 tbsp paprika, 1½ tbsp curry powder, 2 tbsp Italian seasoning and 1 tbsp basil. The handwritten recipe also lists 4 tbsp garlic powder (?), 2 tsp mixed spices, 2 tsp cloves and 2 tsp bay leaves (?). Confirm the question-marked quantities, mixed-spice blend and clove form before using them. The bay leaves are whole, but their count is unconfirmed: do not substitute a guessed leaf count for the written teaspoon amount. Combine the confirmed seasonings in a cup or bowl; keep whole bay leaves and any whole cloves in a food-safe removable spice bag.
  3. Brown the beef and pork. Coat the bottom of a very large pot with oil. Over high heat, cook 5 lb lean ground beef and 5 lb lean ground pork, stirring constantly and breaking up the meat until it loses its red or pink colour. This is the original browning cue; continue through the simmer and check final doneness with a thermometer.
  4. Add the prepared ingredients. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add the prepared 2–3 red bell peppers, 2 large onions, garlic and any extra vegetables, then 3 × 680 mL cans tomato sauce, 3 cans tomato soup (284 mL each assumed), 3 × 156 mL cans tomato paste and 2 × 945 mL containers vegetable juice and all the prepared seasonings (including the spice bag, if used). Add everything promptly and stir after each addition. No extra dilution water is specified in the original.
  5. Simmer gently. Reduce the heat to low and cook for 2–3 hours, stirring occasionally and scraping the bottom to prevent sticking. The original does not specify a lid position. Pot size, lid position and evaporation affect the final yield.
  6. Finish and portion. Add fresh basil as desired. Check that the cooked beef and pork reach at least 71°C / 160°F with a food thermometer; colour alone is not a doneness test. Remove the spice bag and any whole cloves or bay-leaf pieces before serving. Measure the finished sauce when portioning for a reliable yield for your pot.
